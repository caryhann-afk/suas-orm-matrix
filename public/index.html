Civil Air Patrol

sUAS Forms

To install on iPhone/iPad: tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen.

Pick a form to fill out. Each one fills in, signs and prints on the official CAP form.

ORM sUAS Operational Risk Management Matrix Score hazards, get the risk level and approval authority, sign, and print. › 70-5U sUAS Pilot Flight Evaluation CAPF 70-5U — grade S/U/V/NP, endorsements, member and evaluator signatures. › 109U sUAS Sortie Flight Summary & Log CAPF 109U — mission data, targets, and up to 21 flights. › 70-91 Mission Pilot Checkout CAPF 70-91 — grade S/U/V/N/A, result, comments, evaluator signature. ›

Works offline once loaded. Data stays on this device.
